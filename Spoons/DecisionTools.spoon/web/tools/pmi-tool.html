PMI Analysis Guide

About PMI Analysis

PMI (Plus, Minus, Interesting) is a decision-making and brainstorming technique developed by Edward de Bono. It helps you evaluate ideas by systematically considering their positive aspects, negative aspects, and interesting implications.

Unlike simple pros/cons lists, PMI includes an "Interesting" category for aspects that are neither clearly positive nor negative but worth considering.

How to Use

  1. Add Items: Type your thoughts in the input field and press Enter to add to Uncategorized
  2. Categorize: Drag items to Plus (positive), Minus (negative), or Interesting sections
  3. Weight Items: Click the weight badge (1-5) to indicate importance
  4. Review: Check the summary bar at the bottom to see the weighted balance
  5. Decide: Use the analysis to guide your decision

Best Practices

  • Be Thorough: List everything that comes to mind before evaluating
  • Use "Interesting" Wisely: This category is for factors that could go either way or lead to new insights
  • Weight Carefully: Higher weights (4-5) should be reserved for truly significant factors
  • Consider Context: The same factor might be Plus in one situation and Minus in another
  • Review Periodically: Revisit your analysis as circumstances change
  • Export for Reference: Save your analysis for documentation or future review
